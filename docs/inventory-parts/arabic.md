# ARABIC CURRICULUM FOR SECONDARY EDUCATION (SHS 1-3)

- **Source file:** curriculum-sources/Arabic-Curriculum.pdf
- **Subject:** Arabic
- **Level/Form/Class coverage:** SHS 1-3 (document organises content into "YEAR ONE", "YEAR TWO", "YEAR THREE")
- **Issuing authority:** National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA), Ministry of Education, Republic of Ghana (Ghana Education Service also credited, "Enquiries and comments on this Curriculum should be addressed to: The Director-General, National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA), Ministry of Education")
- **Publication year/version:** September 2023 (copyright page: "©2023 National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA)")
- **Document format:** PDF
- **Page count:** Content runs from the cover ("ARABIC | 1") to a final page whose printed footer reads "348 | ARABIC" (Teaching and Learning Resources table, end of Year Three Writing/Creative Writing section — no Year Three "Grammar" sub-strand exists, per the Scope and Sequence table). There is a consistent +2 offset between the PDF's internal page index and the printed page-number footer throughout the document (e.g. PDF index 4 = printed "2 | ARABIC", PDF index 25 = printed "ARABIC | 23"), caused by a cover page, a blank page, and a duplicate/leftover title page before the numbered front matter begins — the same pattern documented in physics.md and robotics.md. Applying that offset, the PDF's own internal page count is approximately 350. This was derived by reading the tool's extracted text directly (page-index vs. printed-footer correspondence), not from separate extraction-tool metadata.
- **Apparent structure (hierarchy levels, in order, exact terminology):** This is a language curriculum and is organised by **language-skill Strand**, not by content-domain strand as in Physics/Robotics.
  1. "THE SHS CURRICULUM OVERVIEW" / "INTRODUCTION" (front matter, shared boilerplate — near-identical to the Physics/Robotics front matter)
  2. "PHILOSOPHY, VISION AND GOAL OF ARABIC" (subject-specific; includes a unique "Contextual Issues" subsection not seen in Physics/Robotics — see Notes)
  3. "ARABIC CURRICULUM DEVELOPMENT PANEL" (writers/reviewers/NaCCA team listing)
  4. "SCOPE AND SEQUENCE" ("Arabic Summary" table of per-Sub-Strand CS/LO/LI counts per year, plus "Overall Totals (SHS 1-3)")
  5. "YEAR ONE" / "YEAR TWO" / "YEAR THREE" (top-level year division, printed as full-page section dividers)
  6. **"Strand"** — always exactly four, in this fixed order every year: "STRAND 1. LISTENING", "STRAND 2. SPEAKING", "STRAND 3. READING", "STRAND 4. WRITING" (labelled "Strand" in the per-table header row, e.g. "Strand 1 LISTENING")
  7. **"Sub-Strand"** (e.g. "Sub-Strand 1 PHONOLOGY OF THE ARABIC LANGUAGE (I)") — the number and names of sub-strands under each Strand *change from year to year* (e.g. Year 1 Listening has 3 sub-strands, Year 2 Listening has only 2; Year 3 Writing has no "Grammar" sub-strand at all — confirmed by the Scope and Sequence table showing "-  -  -" for that cell)
  8. **"Learning Outcomes"** — in Table A of each Sub-Strand, paired with "21st Century Skills and Competencies" and "GESI, SEL and Shared National Values" columns (e.g. "1.1.1.LO.1")
  9. **"Content Standards"** — in Table B of the same Sub-Strand (e.g. "1.1.1.CS.1")
  10. **"Learning Indicators and Pedagogical Exemplars with 21st Century Skills and Competencies, and GESI"** — combined column in Table B, directly under/beside each Content Standard (e.g. "1.1.1.LI.1")
  11. **"Assessment"** — third column of Table B, tied 1:1 to each Learning Indicator (e.g. "1.1.1.AS.1"), using Webb's DoK Levels 1-4
  - Each Sub-Strand ends with a "Teaching and Learning Resources" block.
  - Note: as in Physics/Robotics, Learning Outcomes and Content Standards/Learning Indicators/Assessment are presented in **two separate tables** per Sub-Strand (Table A = LO/21st-Century-Skills/GESI-SEL-Values; Table B = CS/LI+Exemplars/Assessment), sharing the same leading numeric prefix but not a strict 1:1 mapping.
- **Curriculum code format:** `{Year}.{Strand}.{SubStrand}.{Type}.{SequenceNumber}`, e.g. `1.1.1.LO.1` (Learning Outcome), `1.1.1.CS.1` (Content Standard), `1.1.1.LI.1` (Learning Indicator), `1.1.1.AS.1` (Assessment) — identical scheme to Physics and Robotics. First digit = Year (1, 2, or 3), second = Strand number (1=Listening, 2=Speaking, 3=Reading, 4=Writing), third = Sub-Strand number within that Strand/Year. Year 2 and Year 3 codes begin with `2.` and `3.` respectively (e.g. `2.1.1.LO.1`, `3.4.3.CS.2`).
- **Category B elements present:**
  - [x] Exemplars — header "Pedagogical Exemplars", combined into column header "Learning Indicators and Pedagogical Exemplars with 21st Century Skills and Competencies, and GESI"
  - [x] Core Competencies — not labelled "Core Competencies" verbatim; functionally covered under column header "21st Century Skills and Competencies" (Table A), and defined generically in front-matter section of the same name
  - [x] Values — column header "GESI, SEL and Shared National Values" (Table A); a "National Values:" sentence/bullet block appears in almost every Learning Outcome row (e.g. "Tolerance, Social Cohesion responsibility, sense of belongingness, diligence, discipline and patriotism, respect individuals' different beliefs, religion, language and cultures. Have a sense of national identity") — note this differs slightly in wording from Physics's "National Core Values" bullet-list style, though functionally equivalent
  - [x] Pedagogical guidance — embedded as named pedagogies within the Learning Indicators column (e.g. "Collaborative learning:", "Flexible-pace learning:", "Learning Stations:", "Think-Pair Share Activities", "Differentiated Learning:") plus an extensive front-matter section "Learning and Teaching Approaches" and "Universal Design for Learning (UDL) in the SHS Curriculum"
  - [x] Assessment guidance — "Assessment" column citing "Level 1 Recall / Level 2 Skills of conceptual understanding / Level 3 Strategic reasoning / Level 4 Extended critical thinking and reasoning" (Webb's DoK); front matter has the same "Curriculum and Assessment Design: Revised Bloom's Taxonomy and Webb's Depth of Knowledge" section as Physics/Robotics. Unlike Physics, no assessment cells in the sampled sections bold a specific target DoK level — almost all just list the four generic levels verbatim with nothing filled in after the colon
  - [x] Teaching and Learning Resources (TLR) — exact header "Teaching and Learning Resources", listed once per Sub-Strand
  - [x] Suggested Activities — present as bulleted sub-steps inside the named pedagogical-strategy text within the Learning Indicators column (not a separately labelled column/header)
  - [x] Cross-Cutting Themes — not a standalone header; referenced generically in front matter only ("cross-cutting themes such as 21st Century skills, core competencies, the use of ICT, literacy and mathematics, Social Emotional Learning, Gender Equality and Social Inclusion")
  - [x] GESI — exact acronym "GESI" (Gender Equality and Social Inclusion), defined in front matter (page 9 header "Gender Equality and Social Inclusion (GESI)") and appears as a labelled sub-block ("GESI:") inside almost every Learning Outcome row
  - [x] SEL — exact acronym "SEL" (Social Emotional Learning / "Socio-Emotional Learning" both occur), defined with "Five Core Competencies" (Self-Awareness, Self-Management, Social Awareness, Relationship Skills, Responsible Decision-Making) in front matter (pages 11-12); appears as "SEL:" sub-block per Learning Outcome
  - [x] National Values — "Shared National Values" (column header, front matter) / "National Values:" (per-LO block); a "footnote"-style values sub-list also occurs for some Learning Outcomes tied to a specific reading passage (e.g. "The famous Arabic quotations promote the following core values: Contentment, Time Management, Precision/Eloquence, Peaceful Coexistence")
  - [x] 21st Century Skills — exact header "21st Century Skills and Competencies" (Table A column header and front-matter section title); front matter enumerates Critical Thinking and Problem-Solving, Creativity, Collaboration, Communication, Learning for Life, Glocal Citizenship, Systems Thinking, Normative, Anticipatory, Strategic, Self-Awareness competencies — identical list to Physics/Robotics front matter
- **Extraction feasibility:** COMPLETE — native digital PDF, text layer extracts cleanly and is fully searchable, including correctly-encoded Arabic-script example text embedded within the English pedagogical-exemplar prose (poems, proverbs, condolence/congratulation phrases, distance/weight questions — these render as proper Arabic Unicode, not garbled). Caveats: (1) the two footnote markers on page 23 (defining "GESI" and "SEL" abbreviations) are set in a custom/symbol font that does not extract to readable characters — raw extraction yields garbage private-use-area text (e.g. " ϭ 'ĞŶĚĞƌƋƵĂůŝƚǇĂŶĚ^ŽĐŝĂů/ŶĐůƵƐŝŽŶ" instead of "1 Gender Equality and Social Inclusion"); this is recoverable from context (the acronyms are spelled out in full elsewhere in the front matter) but the literal footnote glyphs will not extract correctly with a naive text-layer pull; (2) as in Physics/Robotics, tables occasionally duplicate/interleave text in raw extraction when two physical pages are captured together (observed on printed page 23, where the entire Table A content for LO 1.1.1.LO.1 is repeated twice in sequence); (3) wide multi-column tables (Learning Outcomes | 21st Century Skills | GESI-SEL-Values, and Content Standards | Learning Indicators+Exemplars | Assessment) wrap across page breaks, so automated row-reassociation will need care, same as noted for Robotics.
- **Notes on anything unusual or differing from other subjects:** (1) Structure is organised by **four language-skill strands** (Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing) rather than by content-domain strands as in Physics/Robotics, and — unlike those subjects — the *set of Sub-Strands under a given Strand changes from year to year* (e.g. Year 3 Writing has no "Grammar" sub-strand, confirmed by both the table of contents and the Scope and Sequence table showing "-  -  -" for Content Standards/Learning Outcomes/Learning Indicators in that cell). (2) The front matter (pages 3-20) is near-identical generic "SHS Curriculum Overview" boilerplate shared with Physics and Robotics, down to the same leftover copy-paste artifact: page 3 of this PDF contains a stray title block reading "PHYSICAL AND HEALTH EDUCATION CURRICULUM FOR BASIC 7 – 10 (COMMON CORE PROGRAMME) SEPTEMBER 2020" — the identical artifact found in the Physics and Robotics PDFs, confirming all SHS subject PDFs in this NaCCA batch were produced from a shared template. (3) The "PHILOSOPHY, VISION AND GOAL OF ARABIC" section (page 19) uniquely includes a "Contextual Issues" subsection not seen in Physics/Robotics, listing real implementation challenges in plain prose: "Lack of standardised/unified Arabic curriculum for all schools," "The aim of teaching and learning Arabic are limited to inculcating good morals and ethical conducts," "Misconception of equating Arabic with Islam," "There are no standard textbooks for learners and teaching manuals for teachers," "Lack of resources for the consideration of GESI." (4) The "Scope and Sequence" table (page 21) gives exact totals: 75 Content Standards, 104 Learning Outcomes, 193 Learning Indicators across SHS 1-3. (5) A number of Learning Indicators/exemplars embed real Arabic-script curriculum content as part of the pedagogical instructions themselves (e.g. a sample Q&A "كم كيلومتر من أكرا إلى تمالي؟ / من أكرا إلى تمالي 956 كيلومتر." for distance calculation practice under Strand 2 Speaking; Arabic condolence/congratulation formulae under the same Strand), which is genuine subject content rather than an extraction artifact.
