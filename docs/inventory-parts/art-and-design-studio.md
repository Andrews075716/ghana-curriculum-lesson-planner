- **Notes on anything unusual or differing from other subjects:**
  1. This document uses **"Teaching and Learning Materials"** as the resource-list header, not "Teaching and Learning Resources" (TLR) as used in Physics and Robotics — a genuine terminology difference between subjects that any cross-subject extraction schema should accommodate (e.g. as a normalized "TLR/TLM" field).
  2. The GESI/SEL column header in this document carries **footnote markers** on first use — "GESI¹, SEL² and Shared National Values" with footnotes "1 Gender Equality and Social Inclusion" / "2 Socio-Emotional Learning" at the bottom of the page — a formatting device not observed in the Physics or Robotics samples reviewed.
  3. Same cross-subject copy-paste artifact as seen in Physics and Robotics: the document's early cover/title pages contain overlapping, garbled leftover text from a "PHYSICAL AND HEALTH EDUCATION CURRICULUM FOR BASIC 7 - 10 (COMMON CORE PROGRAMME) SEPTEMBER 2020" template bleeding through the "ART AND DESIGN STUDIO" title/cover page (e.g. raw extracted text: "PHYSICAL AND HEALRTEPUHBLICEODF GUHACNAATION CURRICULUM FOR BASIC 7 - 10 (ACROTMAMNONDCDOERSEIGPRNOGSRTAUMDMIOE)"), confirming this is a shared production artifact across the SHS PDF batch rather than subject-specific.
  4. The "Scope and Sequence" summary table's overall totals (stated in the table, page 23): Content Standards 24, Learning Outcomes 26, Learning Indicators 62, across SHS 1-3.
  5. Pages 11-19 of the front matter (between the GESI/21st-Century-Skills section and the Curriculum Development Panel list) were not read in this pass to stay within the 3-call PDF-reading budget; they likely contain the SEL "Five Core Competencies", "Learning and Teaching Approaches"/UDL, and Bloom's/DoK assessment-framework sections seen in Physics/Robotics, but this was not directly verified here.
  6. Note on tooling: the `Read` tool's PDF-page rendering (`pdftoppm`) was unavailable in this environment, so this inventory was produced using `pdftotext -layout` (via Bash) instead of the `Read` tool's `pages` parameter, while still honoring the "read only 3 targeted page ranges" budget (pages 1-12, pages 23-32, pages 118-123).

Source: page 25 (printed page number "ART AND DESIGN STUDIO | 25"), Year One / Strand 1 / Sub-Strand 1 Learning Outcomes table

```
Subject     ART AND DESIGN STUDIO
Strand      1. ART AND DESIGN THEORIES AND APPLICATION
Sub-Strand  1. MEANINGS, SCOPE AND ROLE OF ART AND DESIGN STUDIO

Learning Outcomes
1.1.1.LO.1
Analyse the scope and role of Art and Design Studios in Ghanaian society.

21st Century Skills and Competencies
Collaboration and Communication; Digital literacy: Learners develop an awareness
of the meaning and major scopes of Art and Design studio practices through
research and dissemination.

GESI1, SEL2 and Shared National Values
GESI: Providing the opportunity for diverse learners with different abilities and
from different backgrounds to actively participate in all lessons inclusively and
using GESI responsive language as pedagogy ensures;
 awareness of personal biases and stereotypes in analysing the scope of common
methods in Art and Design.
 respect and tolerance for an individual's uniqueness and peculiarities
 sensitivity to the interrelatedness of the various spheres of life, groups and
individuals.

National core values: In the selection of art or design works focus should be
placed on national values and attitudes, e.g., patriotism, faithfulness, honesty,
loyalty, discipline, respect, humility, assertiveness, and good citizenship.

1 Gender Equality and Social Inclusion
2 Socio-Emotional Learning
```
