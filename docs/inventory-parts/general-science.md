# GENERAL SCIENCE CURRICULUM FOR SECONDARY EDUCATION (SHS 1-3)

- **Source file:** curriculum-sources/GENERAL-SCIENCE-Curriculum.pdf
- **Subject:** General Science (referred to in its own front matter as "Core Science" — see notes below)
- **Level/Form/Class coverage:** SHS 1-3 ("YEAR ONE", "YEAR TWO", "YEAR THREE")
- **Issuing authority:** National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA), Ministry of Education, Republic of Ghana; Ghana Education Service also credited
- **Publication year/version:** Not independently re-confirmed on the cover page for this file (not in the sampled range), but the identical shared front-matter template strongly implies September 2023, matching every sibling document.
- **Document format:** PDF
- **Page count:** 118 (raw PDF page/form-feed count)
- **Apparent structure (hierarchy levels, in order, exact terminology):** Same Strand → Sub-Strand → Content Standard → Learning Outcome → Learning Indicator → Assessment shape as Physics/Chemistry/Computing, with content-domain (not skill-based) Strands:
  1. **Strand 1. Exploring Materials** — Sub-Strand: Science and Materials in Nature
  2. **Strand 2. Processes For Living** — Sub-Strand: Essentials for Survival
  3. **Strand 3. Vigour Behind Life** — Sub-Strands: Powering the Future with Energy Forms, Forces Acting on Substances and Mechanisms, Consumer Electronics
  4. **Strand 4. Relationships With The Environment** — Sub-Strands: The Human Body and Health, Technology in our Local Industries
- **Curriculum code format:** `{Year}.{Strand}.{SubStrand}.{Type}.{Seq}`, e.g. `1.1.1.LO.1` — identical scheme to every other subject.

  - [x] Core Competencies — folded into "21st-Century Skills and Competencies" (note the hyphenated "21st-Century" here, vs. "21st Century" unhyphenated in Physics/Chemistry/Computing/English/French — another small, real terminology variance)
  - [x] Values — "GESI¹, SEL² and Shared National Values" header, footnoted exactly like other subjects; body uses "SELS" (Social and Emotional Learning Skills) as one acronym variant in this document ("science pedagogy that ensures social and emotional learning skills (SELS)")
  - [x] GESI / [x] SEL / [x] 21st Century Skills — all present
  - [ ] Exemplars / Pedagogical guidance / Assessment guidance / Teaching and Learning Resources / Suggested Activities — not independently confirmed in the sampled pages (front matter + Scope and Sequence + first Learning Outcome block only); expected present by pattern.
  - The front-matter glossary (page 18, "Definition of Key Terms and Concepts in the Curriculum") is present here too, identical wording to the one found in Computing and Agricultural Science — confirming this glossary block is shared boilerplate text reused verbatim across the whole SHS document set, not subject-specific content.
- **Extraction feasibility:** COMPLETE for the sampled pages (native PDF text layer, clean).
- **Notes on anything unusual or differing from other subjects:**
  - The document's own Rationale section (page 20) explicitly calls this subject **"Core Science"** ("The Senior High School Core Science Curriculum has taken into account...") even though the cover/header title and every page footer read "GENERAL SCIENCE" — **the document uses two different names for itself internally.** This should be recorded during extraction (subject name = "General Science" per the cover/footer, but flag the internal "Core Science" alias for administrator awareness — do not silently pick one over the other without noting the discrepancy).
  - Scope and Sequence (page 21-22) gives overall SHS 1-3 totals: **30 Content Standards, 31 Learning Outcomes, 61 Learning Indicators** — the smallest Learning Indicator count of any subject sampled so far, consistent with this being a lighter, compulsory/core-track science subject rather than a specialised elective like Physics/Chemistry/Biology.
  - Same shared front-matter template (SHS Curriculum Overview, glossary, 21st Century Skills, GESI/SEL, Webb's DoK) as every other subject.
