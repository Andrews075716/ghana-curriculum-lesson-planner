- **Notes on anything unusual or differing from other subjects:** The document begins with a very long, generic "THE SHS CURRICULUM OVERVIEW" / "INTRODUCTION" front-matter section (pages 7-21) covering 21st Century Skills, GESI, SEL, Learning and Teaching Approaches, UDL, and the Bloom's/DoK assessment framework — this front matter is almost certainly shared/boilerplate across all SHS subject curricula (the Physical Education front matter fragment even leaked into this PDF's early pages, e.g. "PHYSICAL AND HEALTH EDUCATION CURRICULUM FOR BASIC 7-10 (COMMON CORE PROGRAMME) SEPTEMBER 2020" appearing on page 3 as an apparent copy-paste artifact from another subject's template). Each Learning Outcome row's GESI/SEL text is templated/boilerplate-like across many rows (heavy repetition of near-identical GESI/SEL bullet phrasing), suggesting these were generated from a limited set of stock paragraphs rather than written bespoke per outcome.
