# RELIGIOUS AND MORAL EDUCATION CURRICULUM FOR SECONDARY EDUCATION (SHS 1-3)

- **Source file:** curriculum-sources/RELIGIOUS-AND-MORAL-EDUCATION-Curriculum.pdf
- **Subject:** Religious and Moral Education
- **Level/Form/Class coverage:** SHS 1-3 (document organises content into "YEAR ONE", "YEAR TWO", "YEAR THREE")
- **Issuing authority:** National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA), Ministry of Education, Republic of Ghana (in association with Ghana Education Service)
- **Publication year/version:** September 2023 (as printed on the cover); the PDF's internal document properties record a creation date of 28 August 2024, i.e. the file itself was generated/exported about a year after the printed cover date — likely a later re-export of the same approved 2023 curriculum.
- **Document format:** PDF
- **Page count:** 65 pages (PDF page index/pypdf page count). The highest printed page-footer number found in the body text is "RELIGIOUS AND MORAL EDUCATION | 63", on the last content page — i.e. a roughly 2-page offset between the PDF's own page index and the document's printed footer numbering (cover + rights/imprint page precede the numbered sequence).
- **Apparent structure (hierarchy levels, in order, exact terminology):**
  1. **Strand** (e.g. "STRAND 1. WORK, TIME MANAGEMENT AND LEISURE")
  2. **Sub-Strand** (e.g. "SUB-STRAND 1. WORK, ETHICS AND HONESTY")
  3. **Content Standards** (e.g. "1.1.1.CS.1")
  4. **Learning Outcomes** ("Learning Outcomes" — in a separate table above the Content Standards table, e.g. "1.1.1.LO.1")
  5. **Learning Indicators** (e.g. "1.1.1.LI.1") — presented together with **Pedagogical Exemplars** in one combined column, under the header "Learning Indicators and Pedagogical Exemplars with 21st Century Skills and GESI"
  6. **Assessment** entries tied to each Learning Indicator (e.g. "1.1.1.AS.1"), scored against Webb's DoK Levels 1-4
  - Each Sub-Strand also carries a "Teaching and Learning Resources" block at the end.
  - Note: exactly the same two-table-per-sub-strand layout as Physics/Robotics — Table A = "Learning Outcomes | 21st Century Skills and Competencies | GESI, SEL and Shared National Values"; Table B = "Content Standards | Learning Indicators and Pedagogical Exemplars with 21st Century Skills and GESI | Assessment".
- **Curriculum code format:** `{Year}.{Strand}.{SubStrand}.{Type}.{SequenceNumber}`, e.g. `1.1.1.LO.1` (Learning Outcome), `1.1.1.CS.1` (Content Standard), `1.1.1.LI.1` (Learning Indicator), `1.1.1.AS.1` (Assessment). First digit = Year (1, 2 or 3), second = Strand number, third = Sub-Strand number — identical scheme to Physics/Robotics. At least one numbering typo was observed: the Assessment code under Content Standard `3.3.3.CS.1` is printed as `3.3.1.AS.1` (page 62) instead of the expected `3.3.3.AS.1`.
- **Category B elements present:**
  - [x] Exemplars — header "Learning Indicators and Pedagogical Exemplars with 21st Century Skills and GESI" (Content Standards table column)
  - [x] Core Competencies — not labelled "Core Competencies" verbatim; functionally covered under the column header "21st Century Skills and Competencies" in the Learning Outcomes table
  - [x] Values — header "GESI, SEL and Shared National Values" (LO table) plus a "National Core Values:" bullet list embedded in almost every Learning Outcome row (e.g. Tolerance, Respect, Hard work, Honesty, Selflessness, Co-operation, Perseverance, Contentment, Trustworthy, Integrity)
  - [x] Pedagogical guidance — embedded as named pedagogies inside the Learning Indicators column (e.g. "Exploratory Learning:", "Talk for Learning:", "Activity-Based Learning:", "Enquiry Route Learning:", "General Learning Approach:", "Digital Pedagogy:", "Resource Person:") plus the shared front-matter section "Learning and Teaching Approaches"
  - [x] Assessment guidance — "Assessment" column citing "Level 1 Recall / Level 2 Skills of conceptual understanding / Level 3 Strategic reasoning / Level 4 Extended critical thinking and reasoning" (Webb's DoK-aligned); front matter has the shared section "Curriculum and Assessment Design: Revised Bloom's Taxonomy and Webb's Depth of Knowledge"
  - [x] Teaching and Learning Resources (TLR) — exact header "Teaching and Learning Resources", listed per Content Standard block (e.g. "Religious and Moral Education (RME) Textbook for Basic 7", "Internet", "Computer")
  - [x] Suggested Activities — present as bulleted/named sub-steps inside the Pedagogical Exemplars text, not a separately labelled column
  - [x] Cross-Cutting Themes — not a standalone header; referenced generically in the shared front matter ("cross-cutting themes such as 21st Century skills, core competencies, the use of ICT, literacy and mathematics, Social Emotional Learning, Gender Equality and Social Inclusion")
  - [x] GESI — exact acronym "GESI" (Gender Equality and Social Inclusion), defined in shared front matter (page 8) and appears as a labelled sub-block ("GESI:") inside almost every Learning Outcome row
  - [x] SEL — exact acronym "SEL" (footnoted as "Socio-Emotional Learning" at the foot of the LO table, e.g. page 24: "2 Socio-Emotional Learning"), defined with "Five Core Competencies" in shared front matter (pages 10-11); appears as "SEL:" sub-block per Learning Outcome
  - [x] National Values — "Shared National Values" (front matter) / "National Core Values" (per-LO bullet list)
  - [x] 21st Century Skills — exact header "21st Century Skills and Competencies"; shared front-matter section enumerates the same competency list as Physics/Robotics (Critical Thinking and Problem-Solving, Creativity, Collaboration, Communication, Learning for Life, Glocal Citizenship, Systems Thinking, Normative, Anticipatory, Strategic, Self-Awareness competencies)
- **Extraction feasibility:** COMPLETE — text layer extracts cleanly and is fully searchable (native digital PDF, not scanned). Caveats: (1) as with Physics/Robotics, table cells wrap awkwardly across page breaks in raw extraction, occasionally interleaving text from adjacent pages; (2) at least one numbering typo exists in the source itself (`3.3.1.AS.1` in place of `3.3.3.AS.1`); (3) the cover page's title-block text is badly garbled in raw text extraction because two overlapping title blocks (RME's own title and a leftover Physical and Health Education template title) are interleaved character-by-character in the PDF's text layer (see Notes).
- **Notes on anything unusual or differing from other subjects:** (1) The document opens with the same generic "THE SHS CURRICULUM OVERVIEW" / "INTRODUCTION" front matter (pages 6-17) seen in Physics/Robotics, covering 21st Century Skills, GESI, "Social Emotional Learning (SEL): Five Core Competencies with Examples", Learning and Teaching Approaches, UDL, and the Bloom's/DoK framework — this is almost certainly shared boilerplate across all SHS subject curricula in this batch. (2) The cover page (PDF page 1) shows a much more severe version of the "leftover PHE template" artifact than Physics/Robotics: the extracted text interleaves the RME title with a Physical and Health Education title letter-by-letter, e.g. "PHYSICAL AND HEALRTEPUHBLICEODF GUHACNAATION CURRICULUM" and "RELIG(ICOOUMSMAONN DCOMROE PRRAOLGERDAMUMCEA)TION" — two full title text blocks appear to be stacked directly on top of one another in the PDF, producing garbled interleaved text when extracted (this is worse than the simple duplicated-page artifact noted in physics.md/robotics.md, where the leftover title appeared as a separate intact block). (3) The Scope and Sequence table (page 22, "Religious and Moral Education Summary") gives much smaller totals than Physics/Robotics: only 9 Content Standards, 9 Learning Outcomes, and 19 Learning Indicators across all of SHS 1-3 — the smallest content volume of the three subjects inventoried in this batch. Each of the 3 Strands has only 3 Sub-Strands total spread one-per-year (e.g. Strand 1's "Work, Ethics and Honesty" sub-strand only appears in Year 1, "Time Management" only in Year 2, "Leisure" only in Year 3), rather than every sub-strand recurring across all three years. (4) The Teaching and Learning Resources list for Strand 1/Sub-Strand 1 (Year 1) cites "Religious and Moral Education (RME) Textbook for Basic 7" — a Junior High School ("Basic") level textbook reference appearing inside an SHS curriculum, another likely copy/paste residue from a different curriculum tier's template.
