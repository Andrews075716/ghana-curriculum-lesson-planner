# PERFORMING ARTS CURRICULUM FOR SECONDARY EDUCATION (SHS 1-3)

- **Source file:** curriculum-sources/Performing-Arts-Curriculum.pdf
- **Subject:** Performing Arts
- **Level/Form/Class coverage:** SHS 1-3 ("YEAR ONE", "YEAR TWO", "YEAR THREE")
- **Issuing authority:** National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA), Ministry of Education, Republic of Ghana (front matter panel credits Department of Music Education/Theatre Arts, University of Education, Winneba, as lead contributing institutions)
- **Publication year/version:** Not independently re-confirmed on the cover page for this file (not in sampled range); shared front-matter template implies September 2023.
- **Document format:** PDF
- **Page count:** 175 (raw PDF page/form-feed count)
- **Apparent structure (hierarchy levels, in order, exact terminology):** Same Strand → Sub-Strand → Content Standard → Learning Outcome → Learning Indicator → Assessment shape, three fixed Strands repeated every year:
  1. **Strand 1. Foundations in Artistic Knowledge and Processes** — Sub-Strands: Rudiments in Performing Arts, Skills in Performance, Communications in the Performing Arts, Business Cycle in the Performing Arts
  2. **Strand 2. Artistic Practice** — Sub-Strands: Developing Artistic Ideas and Works, Refining and Completing Artistic Works for Presentation
  3. **Strand 3. Analysis and Interpretation** — Sub-Strands: Critically Responding to One's Own and Others' Creative Works, Connecting Artworks with Life Experiences
  - **Important content-scope finding:** this single curriculum integrates **three art forms together** (Dance, Music, and Drama) rather than having separate curricula per art form — confirmed directly in a Learning Outcome's own text: "Apply the elements of the Performing Arts to develop, compose, and perform artistic product(s) in [DANCE, MUSIC, and DRAMA, or a collaboration])." A single Learning Outcome/Indicator may therefore implicitly branch across three distinct artistic disciplines, which is a genuinely different content-organisation approach from every other subject sampled (none of which mix multiple, otherwise-independent art/subject forms under one Learning Outcome).
  - Document includes **two Appendices** (listed in the TOC as "APPENDIX I" and "APPENDIX II", page ~153+) — the first appendix reference seen in any subject's TOC so far; their content was not sampled and should be checked during full extraction in case they carry additional Category A/B material (e.g. assessment rubrics, resource lists) not captured by the main Strand/Sub-Strand tables.
- **Curriculum code format:** `{Year}.{Strand}.{SubStrand}.{Type}.{Seq}`, e.g. `1.1.1.LO1` (note: printed without a period between "LO" and the sequence digit in this sample — `LO1` not `LO.1` — a source-level formatting inconsistency, to be preserved verbatim rather than silently corrected).
