# GEOGRAPHY CURRICULUM FOR SECONDARY EDUCATION (SHS 1-3)

- **Source file:** curriculum-sources/Geography-Curriculum.pdf
- **Subject:** Geography
- **Level/Form/Class coverage:** SHS 1-3 ("YEAR ONE", "YEAR TWO", "YEAR THREE")
- **Issuing authority:** National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NaCCA), Ministry of Education, Republic of Ghana; Ghana Education Service also credited
- **Publication year/version:** September 2023 (this document's front matter explicitly states the *previous* Geography curriculum dated from "September 2010" and this is its replacement — the replacement's own exact publication date wasn't in the sampled range but is consistent with the shared September-2023 template seen everywhere else)
- **Document format:** PDF
- **Page count:** 126 (raw PDF page/form-feed count)
- **Apparent structure (hierarchy levels, in order, exact terminology):** Same Strand → Sub-Strand → Content Standard → Learning Outcome → Learning Indicator → Assessment shape, with three fixed content-domain Strands repeated every year (same "fixed strand set, stable sub-strands" pattern as English Language, not the "varies by year" pattern seen in Arabic/French):
  1. **Strand 1. The Earth and its Neighbourhoods** — Sub-Strands: The Earth and its Features; Rocks, Weathering, Soil and Mass Wasting; The Earth Atmosphere
  2. **Strand 2. Navigating our Environment** — Sub-Strands: Maps: Their Elements and Analyses; Geospatial Data Collection, Representation and Interpretation
  3. **Strand 3. Human and Environment** — Sub-Strands: Physical Settings and People; Economic Activities; Environmental Degradation; Environmental Hazards and their Management
- **Curriculum code format:** `{Year}.{Strand}.{SubStrand}.{Type}.{Seq}`, e.g. `1.1.1.LO.1` — identical scheme to every other subject.
